Swap ZNN and QSR peer-to-peer: a public Nostr order book, settled by two hash time-locked contracts on Zenon. Self-custodial and escrow-free — your browser wallet signs, and no server ever holds funds. How it works →

01 / Public order book

ZNN / QSR

Post an order

Your on-chain balance is checked before the signed order is published.

An order is a public signed intention. Funds move only when both HTLCs exist on chain. zwap converts the amount and price to exact integers in minor units before signing, and shows you both figures here.

Nostr carries the market and the encrypted coordination. Two HTLCs sharing one hash carry the value: neither side can take one leg without releasing the other.

05 / Local data

Trade journal and order keys

Open swaps, pending order publications, and the per-order Nostr keys that sign your listings live only in this browser. Erasing them abandons any swap in progress.
